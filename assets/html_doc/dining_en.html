Pine Garden Cafe

Pine Garden Cafe was originally a corridor-style building constructed between the 17th and 18th years of the Showa era. It previously served as a dormitory for Japanese military choresmen.

Today, it has been transformed into the Pine Garden Herb Dining, combining historical relics with modern senses. Various herbs are incorporated into meals and beverages, featuring unique fresh pine needle juice and pine needle coffee. This experience allows travelers not only to visually appreciate the century-old beauty of Pine Garden but also to deeply enjoy this unique and relaxing time through their taste buds.
