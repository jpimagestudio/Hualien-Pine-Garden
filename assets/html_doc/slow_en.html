The Faint Cultural Light Spreading Through the Greenery

The Slow Living Pavilion is a creative and sales platform specially built for local artists in Hualien. We are dedicated to supporting the cross-disciplinary integration of local artisans, designers, and cultural and creative practitioners. Here, you can see the artistic crystallization of diverse fields, from weaving and handcrafts to woodworking and metalwork. Every piece carries the warmth of Hualien's land and the persistence of its craftsmen.

In the colorful display area, the miniature magnets and various small items of "Taiwanese Lifestyle" immediately catch the eye. Those designs that condense Taiwanese street scenes, retro shop signs, and daily memories into a tiny square, gently illuminated by spotlights, resemble a miniature exhibition of time.

Delving into a corner of the space, playful magnets featuring traditional indigenous clothing are hung. Their vibrant totems and delicate colors silently echo the unique multicultural pulse of Eastern Taiwan. Alongside them, warm wooden blessing ornaments and Daruma-shaped keychains transform profound cultural heritage into light, everyday companionship. In this thoughtfully curated space, every object seems to softly remind travelers to slow down and savor the traces of passing time.
