Time-Space Bunker: A Documentary of the Underground Shelter

This underground air raid shelter was built between 1942 and 1943. It is the darkest and heaviest wartime witness in the history of Pine Garden.

Step 1
Bunker Entrance
Solid terrazzo steps extend downwards. This was the core refuge and command space for high-ranking military officials when facing severe bombings by the US military.
Step 2
Inside the Observation Tunnel
The interior preserves mottled brick walls and the remains of blast-proof iron doors up to four centimeters thick. A heavy wartime atmosphere condenses in the air.
Step 3
Taiwan Kamikaze Documentary: The Tragic Farewell of the White Butterflies
In Taiwan during the late stages of the Pacific War, special attack members were called "White Butterflies". This documentary record begins on January 15, 1944, and lasts until June 7, 1945, with a total of 3461 sorties dispatched across Taiwan.
Step 4
The Birth of the Kamikaze Special Attack Unit
In 1944, after Japan's defeat in the Battle of Midway, Lieutenant General Takijiro Onishi proposed the crazy concept of "one plane for one ship".
